# 2509.25531v5 — MixtureVitae: Open Web-Scale Pretraining Dataset with High Quality Instruction and Reasoning Data Built from Permissive-First Text Sources

**Relevance:** low
This is a pretraining-corpus construction paper (licensing-focused data curation for training models from scratch at 130M–1.7B params); our project post-trains an already-pretrained/instruct 1.2B model for tool-calling reliability and quantized deployment, so nothing here is directly actionable, though the instruction/agent-data mixing and IFEval decontamination work is adjacent.

## TL;DR

MixtureVitae is a permissive-licensed pretraining corpus (public-domain, CC-BY/Apache, government works, EU TDM-eligible sources) that deliberately mixes in a large share of synthetic instruction, reasoning, math, and code data normally reserved for post-training, then trains from-scratch models at 130M–1.7B params on 50B/300B token budgets to show this mix beats other permissive corpora on MMLU, GSM8K, MBPP, and IFEval. It's a data-curation and pretraining paper, not a post-training or quantization paper, so it doesn't touch our project's actual levers (LoRA/DPO/GRPO, tool-call schema validity, GGUF quantization).

## Techniques to try

- None directly transferable — the paper's contribution is a full-corpus construction strategy for training models from scratch, not a fine-tuning or quantization recipe applicable to an already-instruct-tuned 1.2B model.

## Baselines & datasets used

- Instruction/agent-related pretraining components that could inform a data-mix ablation if this project ever needed synthetic agent-trace data: "agent-focused instructions from OpenManus-RL (Ulab-UIUC and MetaGPT, 2024)" and a "Permissive (AgentTraj-L, Agent-FLAN, etc)" subset, noted as using "GPT-4 used for generating traces in the AgentTraj-L subset" (Table of Tier composition, App.).
- Compares against other permissive-only corpora (Common Corpus, DCLM, FineWeb-Edu, HPLT-2.0, Nemotron-CC-HQ, SlimPajama) at 50B and 300B token budgets, 130M–1.7B model sizes — useful only as background on permissive-data pretraining, not as post-training baselines.

## Pitfalls / negative results

- Reports that permissive-only human-text corpora underperform on reasoning/instruction-following: "our experiments (Section 3) show that models trained on them can lag in complex reasoning, math, and instruction following, suggesting that strictly permissive human text alone is insufficient to instill these advanced skills" — relevant only as motivation for why synthetic instruction/agent data matters, not applicable to our post-training setting since we start from an already-instructed model.
- IFEval-adjacent finding is about training-data contamination, not model quality: 13-gram contamination scan found "341 554" contaminated docs / a high per-benchmark contamination rate for IFEval among the tested benchmarks, and decontaminated eval set sizes are reported (IFEval original 541 examples reduced to "429" after removing overlapping examples) — a reminder that IFEval numbers reported by pretraining/post-training papers should be checked for contamination, but this doesn't bear on our project's own IFEval guardrail measurement methodology directly.

## Metric / eval choices

- Uses standard benchmark suite (MMLU, ARC, HellaSwag, BoolQ, PIQA, Winogrande, GSM8K, MBPP/MBPP+, IFEval, GPQA) evaluated at 130M–1.7B scales — overlaps with our project's guardrail metrics (IFEval, MMLU-Pro, GPQA Diamond) only in benchmark name, not in a way that supplies a comparable number since architectures/training regimes are unrelated (from-scratch pretraining vs. post-training an instruct model).
- No BFCL, tool-calling, structured-output, or quantization evaluation of any kind appears in the paper.

## Open questions for our project

- None of this paper's contamination-decontamination methodology or data-mix findings resolve any of the open questions listed in the reader brief (quantization vs. structured output, flag-vs-assert training signal, throughput floor, vocab size effects, reward hacking, LoRA vs. full fine-tuning) — the paper operates one stage upstream (pretraining data) of everything this project's ladder addresses.
