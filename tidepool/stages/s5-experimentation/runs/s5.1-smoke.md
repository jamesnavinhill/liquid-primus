## Attempt 1 — both jobs FAILED on the last line, after passing every check

Both jobs completed all of their work, saved every artifact, and then crashed on the same
statement: `lab.finish("success", ..., score=score)`. The SDK's `finish` takes
`(message, score, ...)` and has no status argument, so `"success"` bound to `message`, the
description bound positionally to `score`, and the explicit `score=` was a duplicate.

    TypeError: Lab.finish() got multiple values for argument 'score'

**Failure signature:** `TypeError: Lab.finish() got multiple values for argument 'score'`,
raised at the final status call, after `update_progress(100)` and after every
`save_artifact`. Identical in both jobs.

What that cost and what it did not. Every artifact saves before the call, so no measurement
was lost: the supervised run's five artifacts (`adapter.zip`, `metrics.json`, `score.json`,
`train_history.json`, `mix_calibration.json`) and the replay run's three (`replay.jsonl`,
`replay_summary.json`, `score.json`) are all listed by `lab job artifacts` and were read back
to recover the numbers below. What was lost is the job record: both read `FAILED` with
`job_data.score` empty, so neither appears in a `lab job list --score-metric` dashboard.

## Attempt 2 — the fix, and why it is being re-run rather than waived

`lab.finish(message=..., score=...)` on success and `lab.error(message=...)` on assertion
failure, in both `main.py` files, with the SDK's real signature read off the published
package rather than inferred. Failed runs now carry their headline figures in the message,
since `error` takes no score; the numbers were already in `score.json` either way.

The science had passed, so re-running is not about the result. It is about what happens next:
the projected reference run is 16 hours, the baselines and the sweep after it are ranked by
`lab job list --score-metric`, and a crash on the last line empties exactly the field that
ranking reads. Catching that before the expensive half is what this substage is for, and a
patch that has never executed is not yet a fix. Two more L4 smokes at roughly 0.1 GPU-hour
each buy a proven path.
