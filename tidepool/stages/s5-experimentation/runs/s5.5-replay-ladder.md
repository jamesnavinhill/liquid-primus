## What it is

s5.4 falsified H2 and named the IFEval regression as the binding constraint. Replay was the
axis meant to protect general quality and it stalled after 2 to 3 points, because the frozen
buffer was sampled proportionally from a pool whose constraint-bearing slice is 10.5%, which
put verifiable-constraint instruction following at 0.1% to 0.5% of the training tokens. Dose
and composition were never separable in the C sweep because only dose ever moved. This run
separates them, at C7's recipe and the same fixed 64M-token budget every C arm got.

| arm | script | ceiling | waits on | replay | buffer |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| RBP | `replay.py` | 18 GB | — | — | 32,000 prompts, proportional (`strata {}`) |
| RBC | `replay.py` | 18 GB | — | — | 32,000 prompts, 0.47 autoif + 0.03 ifstruct |
| R1 | `main.py` | 9.5 GB | RBC | 0.05 | reweighted |
| R2 | `main.py` | 9.5 GB | RBP | 0.20 | proportional |
| R3 | `main.py` | 9.5 GB | RBC | 0.20 | reweighted |

Contrasts: **R3 − R2** isolates composition at a fixed dose, **R3 − R1** isolates dose over
one and the same buffer, **R1 − C7** asks whether a small reweighted dose moves IFEval at all.
C7 is the origin: raw mixture, rank 16, no replay, detection 0.7222.

Peak concurrent demand 37.0 GB of a 43.9 GB limit (RBP still generating while RBC has released
R1 and R3). Estimate 11 to 12 GPU-hours, stated in the stage report before submission.

## Why the reweighted shares are 0.47/0.03 and not 0.45/0.05

The decontaminated pool holds 46,734 `open_perfectblend_autoif` rows and 1,279
`ifstruct_train_generated` rows. IFStruct is one of this project's evaluation sets, so the s4
contamination filter dropped 18,721 of that sub-source's 20,000 training rows. At 32,000
prompts a 0.05 share would ask for 1,600 against 1,279 available and the sampler would fail
the run. The buffer is still exactly half constraint-bearing.

## Pass condition

IFEval `prompt_level_strict` within 2.0 of B1, detection at or above C7's 0.7222, false-flag
at or below 0.15. Failing that, the forced trade-off is the paper's result.
