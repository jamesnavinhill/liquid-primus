## What it is

s5.4 falsified H2 and named the IFEval regression as the binding constraint. Replay was the
axis meant to protect general quality and it stalled after 2 to 3 points, because the frozen
buffer was sampled proportionally from a pool whose constraint-bearing slice is 10.5%, which
put verifiable-constraint instruction following at 0.1% to 0.5% of the training tokens. Dose
and composition were never separable in the C sweep because only dose ever moved. This run
separates them, at C7's recipe and the same fixed 64M-token budget every C arm got.

|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| RBP | `replay.py` | 18 GB | — | — | 32,000 prompts, proportional (`strata {}`) |
| RBC | `replay.py` | 18 GB | — | — | 32,000 prompts, 0.47 autoif + 0.03 ifstruct |

Contrasts: **R3 − R2** isolates composition at a fixed dose, **R3 − R1** isolates dose over
one and the same buffer, **R1 − C7** asks whether a small reweighted dose moves IFEval at all.
C7 is the origin: raw mixture, rank 16, no replay, detection 0.7222.

Peak concurrent demand 37.0 GB of a 43.9 GB limit (RBP still generating while RBC has released
R1 and R3). Estimate 11 to 12 GPU-hours, stated in the stage report before submission.

## Why the reweighted shares are 0.47/0.03 and not 0.45/0.05

The decontaminated pool holds 46,734 `open_perfectblend_autoif` rows and 1,279
`ifstruct_train_generated` rows. IFStruct is one of this project's evaluation sets, so the s4
contamination filter dropped 18,721 of that sub-source's 20,000 training rows. At 32,000
prompts a 0.05 share would ask for 1,600 against 1,279 available and the sampler would fail
the run. The buffer is still exactly half constraint-bearing.

## Pass condition

IFEval `prompt_level_strict` within 2.0 of B1, detection at or above C7's 0.7222, false-flag
at or below 0.15. Failing that, the forced trade-off is the paper's result.

### The pass condition fails on all three arms

IFEval `prompt_level_strict`, against B1's 0.8189: R1 0.7689 (−5.00), R2 0.7689 (−5.00),
R3 0.7560 (−6.29). The allowance is 2.0. The paired test puts a real interval behind it:
B1 − R3 is +0.0628 [+0.0351, +0.0906], Holm p = 5.9e-4, so even the optimistic end of the
interval is 3.51 points, well past the allowance. Detection is held or nearly held (C7 0.7222;
R3 0.7148, R2 0.7111, R1 0.6148) and false-flag is nowhere near its ceiling (3, 1 and 2 items
of 138), so the condition fails on the IFEval half alone.

### What the ladder actually shows: the axis is inert

The three pre-registered contrasts all come back null on the metric they were written for.

| contrast | isolates | IFEval delta | 95% CI | Holm p | reads as |
|---|---|--:|---|--:|---|
| R2 − R3 | composition at a fixed 0.20 dose | +0.0129 | [−0.0074, +0.0333] | 1 | indistinguishable |
| R1 − R3 | dose over one and the same buffer | +0.0129 | [−0.0111, +0.0370] | 1 | indistinguishable |
| C7 − R3 | replay against no replay at all | −0.0222 | [−0.0573, +0.0129] | 1 | indistinguishable |

Reweighting the buffer to half constraint-bearing does not move IFEval. Quadrupling the dose
over that buffer does not move IFEval. Replay against no replay does not move IFEval. The
only arm in the family that separates on IFEval is B1, the untrained base, and it separates
upward from every one of them.

The mechanism reading from the score cards agrees. Constraint-bearing token share is about
2.5% for R1 (0.05 × 0.50), about 2.1% for R2 (0.20 × 0.105) and 10% for R3 (0.20 × 0.50).
R1 and R2 sit within half a point of each other on that share and land on *identical* IFEval
to four decimals; R3 carries four times more and scores lowest of the three. The effect
saturates near 2% of training tokens and does not improve past it. s5.4 diagnosed the sweep's
replay as a dose two orders of magnitude too small; the ladder raised it by two orders of
magnitude and the metric did not respond, which retires the diagnosis rather than confirming
it.

### What replay does do, and it is not free

Three cells survive family correction on the C7 − R3 and R2 − R3 contrasts, and they are all
about something other than instruction following.

- **Structured output pays for it.** C7 − R3 on IFStruct validity is +0.0515
  [+0.0360, +0.0670], Holm p = 3.5e-9. R1 0.0820, R2 0.0850, R3 0.0885 against C7's 0.1400
  and B1's 0.1355: every replay arm gives back about five points of first-attempt schema
  validity that C7 held at the base's level. Against the project's `+5.0 over matched base`
  structured-output criterion, C7 was already only +0.45; the replay arms are −4.70 to −5.35.
- **Text-convention tool calling improves.** C7 − R3 on `bfcl_text` is −0.0284
  [−0.0381, −0.0186], Holm p = 8.4e-7, and R2 − R3 is −0.0172, Holm p = 4.6e-3. R3 is the
  best arm the project has on the calling convention s4 defined, and the gain is ordered by
  constraint-bearing share, which is the one place the dose axis behaves as designed.
- **A small dose costs detection.** R1 − R3 on `probes_flag_detect` is −0.1000
  [−0.1556, −0.0444], Holm p = 0.019: 29 fewer probes flagged of 270. R2 and R3, at four
  times the dose, are indistinguishable from C7. The displacement confound s5.5 stated up
  front predicted the opposite ordering, that more replay would cost more detection, and the
  data invert it.

BFCL composites: R3 0.7136, C7 0.7049, R1 0.7034, R2 0.7011. On the paired test R3 beats C7
on the text convention and is indistinguishable from it on native, so R3 is the ladder's
tool-calling winner on both the ranking metric and the evidence gate.

### Verdict and the checkpoint choice

The pass condition fails, so s5.4's fallback governs: the forced trade-off is the paper's
result, and the delivered checkpoint is chosen on the operator's stated priority order with
tool calling first. That selects **R3** — highest BFCL composite, the only arm with a
family-corrected tool-calling gain over C7, detection statistically level with C7, IFEval
indistinguishable from C7 and from the other two rungs.

Recorded plainly, because the rule does not hide it: R3 is selected while carrying the only
family-corrected regression in the ladder, −5.15 points of IFStruct validity against C7. C7
is retained as the fallback checkpoint into s5.6 for exactly that reason. s5.6 should try to
recover structured-output validity on top of R3's recipe; if it cannot, the delivered model
and the reported trade-off both have to name which criterion was sacrificed.

The falsification is the substantive finding. H2 died at s5.4 on the IFEval regression, and
replay was the axis named to rescue it. The ladder separates dose from composition, moves
each by the largest factor the token budget allows, and neither moves the metric. The
IFEval cost of this specialization is not a replay-tuning problem; it is a property of the
training mixture, and s6 should report it as one.

## Departures and decisions recorded

1. **`--force` overwrite of `score.json` in `tidepool/s5.3/arms/R{1,2,3}/`.** Those objects
   already existed. They were downloaded and inspected before anything was overwritten: the
   bytes were the *training* job's score cards, not eval cards, so no recorded comparison
   depended on them. They are preserved at `runs/s5.5/train-cards.json` and the promotion
   then ran with `--force`.
2. **B1 promoted probes-only.** B1's clean-corpus re-score replaced only
   `scored_probes.jsonl`, leaving in place the older full-pass `eval_summary.json`, which
   carries no corpus probe rate. The comparison driver notices and says so in its own notes:
   `B1 has no score.json in storage, so any rate it carries is not cross-checked`. B1's
   probe cells in this family are therefore uncross-checked, and its BFCL, IFEval and
   IFStruct cells come from the full pass and are cross-checked as usual. Determinism was
   verified first, as in s5.3: 464 rows shared between the old and new probe files, zero
   verdict changes on `correct` and on `detail.flagged`, 138 new-only corpus items.
3. **R3 as the comparison reference, so R1 − C7 is not family-corrected here.** The compare
   driver takes one reference per run, and its own header forbids splitting arms across two
   runs because Holm would then correct each half against a family half the size. Two of the
   three pre-registered contrasts are written against R3, so R3 was chosen and the third,
   R1 − C7, is read from the score cards rather than from a corrected cell. It is +3.51
   points of IFEval, which is inside the C7 − R3 interval and does not change the verdict.
4. **B4 failed and was not rerun in place.** `08dab391` reports FAILED on its third arm:
   Granite 4.0-1b hit `torch.OutOfMemoryError` in `granitemoehybrid` rotary embedding with
   13.62 GB free on the card but only 5.05 GB allowed under its 5.5 GB share of
   `pack_gb = 11,5.5,5.5`. The ceiling is the cause, not the card. B4 is a supplementary
   clean-arm re-score of a baseline and is off the critical path, so it is requeued as
   filler at a larger ceiling instead of blocking this substage.
